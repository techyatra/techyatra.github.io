Your portfolio site — at a glance

Everything runs in the browser and on free hosting. There is no custom server: the page calls public APIs to show your blog, videos, and to save form data and light analytics.

Read this like a story
  • Green (GitHub Pages) — hosts your page; it does not run a database, only files.
  • Blue (Hashnode) — powers the blog list and article content your visitors see.
  • Red / orange (YouTube + helper) — RSS gives video IDs; the API adds length so you can show the right videos.
  • Teal (Supabase) — stores newsletter signups, contact messages, read counts, and visit counts. Your app uses a public “anon” key; safety comes from rules you set in Supabase.
